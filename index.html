MS in Computer Science Tracker

Please note that this tool is provided for student planning and convenience only. It is not an official degree audit. Students may use it to review their progress toward the MS in Computer Science (MSCS) and Professional MS in Computer Science (MSCPS) requirements and to plan future coursework.

Start here: pick your degree in the tabs below, then pick your pathway. Each pathway has its own requirements, timeline, and forms. The FAQs apply to every pathway.

Grade requirement: professional development, breadth (BIN), Projects, research, and subplan courses must all be completed with a grade of B or higher. Electives are the exception — they may count with a lower grade. A breadth course finished with a C, C+, or B- can still count as an Elective. Whatever you take, your cumulative GPA must stay at or above 3.00.
